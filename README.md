## Construction, Engineering & Technical Services

### Who We Are

Sanogo Group & Co. is a construction and technical services company based in Abidjan, Côte d’Ivoire. The company provides comprehensive construction, renovation, engineering, maintenance, and technical solutions for residential, commercial, and other development projects.

We combine local expertise, technical knowledge, professional project management, and a commitment to quality to deliver reliable solutions tailored to our clients' needs.

Our activities cover a wide range of construction and related technical services, allowing us to support projects from planning and execution to renovation, maintenance, and technical assistance.

### What We Do

#### 1. Building Construction

We undertake new construction projects of different sizes and types, providing professional solutions for residential, commercial, and other buildings.

Our services cover the different stages of construction, from project preparation and technical planning to execution and completion.

#### 2. Major & Secondary Works

We provide both major structural works and secondary building works, ensuring that every part of a project is handled with attention to quality, functionality, and durability.

#### 3. Rehabilitation & Renovation

We rehabilitate and renovate existing buildings to improve their functionality, safety, appearance, and overall value.

Our rehabilitation services can include structural repairs, modernization, improvements, and adaptation of existing spaces to meet new requirements.

#### 4. Roads & Networks (VRD)

We provide VRD — *Voirie et Réseaux Divers* — services related to roads, infrastructure networks, site development, and associated works.

These services help prepare and connect construction sites with the infrastructure required for effective development.

#### 5. Land Development

We provide land development and site preparation solutions designed to support construction and real-estate projects.

#### 6. Construction Materials & Equipment

We are also involved in the sale and supply of construction materials and equipment, helping clients and project teams access the resources required for their construction activities.

#### 7. Technical Assistance & Consulting

Our team provides technical assistance, advice, and support to help clients make informed decisions throughout their projects.

We can assist with project-related technical requirements, planning, and practical construction considerations.

#### 8. Real Estate Services

Our activities also include real-estate management and related services, supporting property owners and clients with their real-estate needs.

#### 9. Maintenance Services

We provide building and facility maintenance solutions designed to preserve the performance, safety, and functionality of properties and technical installations.

#### 10. Cleaning & Facility Services

We provide professional cleaning and related maintenance services for buildings and facilities, helping maintain clean, functional, and professional environments.

#### 11. Fire Safety & Protection

We provide fire-safety solutions, including fire extinguishers, fire protection networks, detection systems, and related safety installations.

#### 12. Electronic Security

Our technical services include electronic security solutions such as video surveillance and access-control systems, helping improve the security of buildings and facilities.

#### 13. Generator Installation

We provide generator installation services to support reliable power supply and backup-power requirements for different types of facilities.

#### 14. Industrial Electricity

We provide electrical services for industrial environments, including installation and technical support for electrical systems.

#### 15. Industrial Maintenance

We provide industrial maintenance services designed to help organizations maintain the reliability, efficiency, and operational performance of their equipment and installations.

#### 16. IT & Technical Equipment

Our activities also include the supply of computer equipment, office equipment, and related technical services.

### Our Approach

At Sanogo Group & Co., we believe that successful construction is built on strong planning, technical expertise, quality workmanship, responsible project management, and clear communication.

We work to understand each client's requirements and provide practical, efficient, and durable solutions.

### Our Commitment

Our commitment is to deliver quality construction and technical services while maintaining professionalism, reliability, safety, and customer satisfaction throughout every project.

Whether it is a new construction project, a rehabilitation project, infrastructure work, technical installation, maintenance requirement, or a specialized service, Sanogo Group & Co. aims to provide dependable solutions adapted to each client's needs.

### Our Service Areas

Based in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire, Sanogo Group & Co. provides construction and related technical services in Abidjan and surrounding areas. The company's stated vision is to deliver construction services and technical expertise across projects of different types and sizes.
